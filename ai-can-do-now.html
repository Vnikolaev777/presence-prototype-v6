What still needs humans

AI drafts, humans approve or own

Legal sign-off

human

Datenschutzerklärung and Impressum drafts must be reviewed by counsel or the Gemeinde's data-protection officer before publication. AI gets you to 90%; the last mile is liability.

Manual a11y validation

human

Keyboard-only traversal, screen-reader pass, real users with impairments. Automated tools catch ~30% of WCAG issues; the rest needs lived experience.

CMS / hosting changes

vendor

Escola is a managed CMS. Header changes, asset bundling pipelines, sitemap auto-generation likely require Escola's cooperation or a config request. AI can prepare the exact changeset; the vendor must apply it.

Editorial decisions on IA

human

AI proposes a parent-task-oriented IA, but the school's communications team has to live with it and own the migrations. Worth one workshop, not 20 emails.

Gated / authenticated areas

school

The Eltern-Login, staff area, and Mein Escola dashboards are out of reach without credentials. The most sensitive student-data handling lives there; AI can audit only the public surface.

DNS, email, infrastructure

ops

SPF/DMARC/DKIM/CAA/MTA-STS records, TLS configuration, edge-CDN setup. AI can spec the records; only the domain owner and ISP can apply them.

How "ship today / week / sprint" was scored

Ship today means a single agent run produces text or a one-line edit ready to paste in. No crawl, no deploy, no orchestration. Confidence is high because the input is the audit itself and the output is well-bounded prose or a single attribute.

Ship this week means one structured pass over the site or a known list of pages — alt text, structured data, sitemap generation, image optimisation. Needs file access or build access, not human review of every output.

Ship this sprint means crawl + analyse + produce → human review → deploy. PDF tagging, asset bundling, IA redesign. The agent does the analysis and the diff; humans gate the deploy.

The "+38 score lift" projection assumes every Ship-Today and Ship-This-Week item lands and roughly half of Ship-This-Sprint. It is a directional figure, not a contractual one — Performance lift in particular depends on what the bundling actually removes, which can only be known once the dependency graph is mapped.
